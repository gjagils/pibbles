Support

Stuck? We're here.

A bug, an idea for a new puzzle, or a puzzle that seems wrong: we read every message.

Email us at

hello@pibbles.app

It helps to mention your phone model, the date of the puzzle and which kind it was.

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and the puzzle maker checks that before a puzzle goes out.

Why is Sunday so much harder than Monday?

On purpose. Puzzles start small on Monday and grow a little every day, so Sunday is the big one.

How do streaks and jokers work?

Solve at least one puzzle a day to keep your streak. Every few days in a row earns a joker, which saves your streak on a day you miss.

How is the ranking worked out?

Your time, plus 30 seconds for each hint. Lowest wins. Only your first solve of each puzzle counts, so take your time.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The puzzles are made on your phone. You only need a connection to post to the ranking.

Can my scores be removed?

Of course. Email us your display name and the dates. See the privacy policy for what we store.
