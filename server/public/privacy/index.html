Privacy policy

Your puzzles, your business

Last updated 22 November 2026

Pibbles is a daily puzzle app. We built it to need as little about you as possible. There are no accounts, no tracking and no advertising. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone and why.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, streak, jokers, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. The daily puzzles themselves are worked out on your phone, so the app also works offline.

What is sent when you post a score

When you finish a puzzle and your result goes to the daily ranking, the app sends:

  • A random device id. The app makes up this id the first time it runs. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email, Apple or Google account, or advertising id. It only lets the ranking count one result per device per puzzle.
  • The display name you chose (or "Player" if you did not choose one). It is shown on the public ranking, so please do not use your full name if you would rather not.
  • Your result: the date, the puzzle kind, your time, the number of hints you used, and your answer, so the server can check the puzzle was really solved.

The app may also download the day's puzzles from our server. That request contains only the date.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or passwords.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other data from your phone.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no advertising id.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Where it is stored

Scores are stored in a database at Cloudflare, which hosts pibbles.app. Our database does not store IP addresses. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address briefly to deliver the request and protect the service from abuse, under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Daily rankings are kept so you can look back at past days. If you want your scores removed, write to us with your display name and the dates, and we will delete them.

Children

Pibbles is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. A display name is optional.

Advertising later

Pibbles has no ads today. If we ever add advertising or in-app purchases, we will update this page first and the app will ask for any permission the law requires.
